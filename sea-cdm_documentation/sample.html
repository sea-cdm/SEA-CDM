Sample

A sample is the result of an experiment that is collected from an organism and used to generate data. A biological sample (biosample) refers to the tissue, cell culture, or biological product collected from an organism, while an experimental sample (expsample) is the sample that has been further processed. The Sample class has 4 primary attributes: biosample collection, biosample type, collection time, expsample type.

Collection time is stored in five different variables, depending on how Sample data was collected.
