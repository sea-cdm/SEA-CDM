Contact Us

SEA-CDM is developed and maintained by the He Lab at the University of Michigan Medical School. Reach out using one of the channels below depending on your question.

Bug Reports & Feature Requests

Open an issue on the SEA-CDM GitHub issue tracker. This is the preferred way to report problems with the data model or documentation.

Source Code & Repositories

Browse the code and related projects on the SEA-CDM GitHub organization, including the SEA-CDM, OSEAN-DB, and OSEAN-KG repositories.

General Inquiries

For questions about collaboration, publications, or the SEA-CDM project in general, email the He Lab at yongqunh@umich.edu.
